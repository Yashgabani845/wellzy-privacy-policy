Privacy Policy

App: Arrow Sweep: Escape Puzzle

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Sweep: Escape Puzzle ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and protects information when you play our mobile game.

Summary: Arrow Sweep is designed as a private, offline-friendly logic puzzle game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, or physical address.

1. Information We Collect

A. Local Gameplay Data

To provide core game progression, your device locally stores:

  • Level completion status and stars earned.
  • Current daily challenge streak and completion records.
  • Game settings (audio volume, haptics, theme preferences).
  • In-game booster inventories (Hints, Undos, Shuffles).

This data is stored solely on your local device and is never uploaded to any proprietary personal database.

B. Automated Data & Advertising (Third-Party Services)

To keep the game free to play, Arrow Sweep uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements. Google and its ad partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Anonymous Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution).
  • General diagnostic and crash analytics data.
  • Coarse location data (country/city level) to serve relevant advertisements.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

Our application integrates third-party services that have their own privacy policies governing their collection and use of data:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy & How Google uses information from sites or apps.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Save and restore your puzzle progress and streaks.
  • Deliver non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • Reward players with in-game lives or boosters upon watching rewarded ads.
  • Monitor app stability, detect bugs, and ensure smooth 60 FPS performance.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Sweep: Escape Puzzle is designed for a general audience of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through third-party services without parental consent, please contact us immediately.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's cache and data in your device's Android Settings, or by uninstalling the application.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
