Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, users of Wellzy: Health & Wellness have full control to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

1. In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can delete your account and all associated data directly inside the app at any time:

  1. Open the Wellzy app.
  2. Go to the Profile screen and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap the Delete Account button and confirm.

Result: Your account and all stored health data will be deleted immediately and permanently from our Firebase servers.

2. Web / Email Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app or have uninstalled it, you can submit a web deletion request below. We will delete your account and all data associated with your registered email address promptly upon receipt.

Request Account Deletion via Email

Or email us directly at: wellzy.fit@gmail.com

3. Data That Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is executed, all of the following data associated with your user account is permanently deleted:

  • Account Profile: Registered email, user ID, display name, age, gender, height, and weight goals.
  • Calorie & Food Logs: All logged meals, nutritional records, custom food items, and daily calorie targets.
  • Health Tracking Records: Water intake logs, weight tracking history, and reminder preferences.
  • Authentication Sessions: All OAuth tokens and user authentication records.

4. Data Retention

We do not retain any copy, backup, or archive of your personal data after deletion is completed. Once deleted, the data is permanently erased and cannot be restored.

Support & Inquiries
